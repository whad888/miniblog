# How to Pick the Right Solar Company to Install Solar Power on My Roof in the Philippines

The Philippines enjoys an average of 5.3 hours of sunshine daily, providing homeowners with a unique opportunity to harness solar energy. This can significantly lower electricity bills and increase property value. However, the first step toward sustainable energy is choosing the right solar company.

## 1. Understanding the Solar Landscape in the Philippines
The solar market in the Philippines is expanding rapidly, fueled by government incentives and rising awareness of renewable energy's benefits. The nation aims to increase its renewable energy share to 35% by 2030, creating a promising environment for solar investments. However, not all solar companies are equal. To maximize your return on investment (ROI), understanding the landscape and identifying reputable providers is essential.

## 2. The 5-Step Blueprint for Choosing a Solar Company
Finding the right solar company involves a structured approach. Here’s a five-step blueprint to guide your decision:

1. **Research Local Providers**: Begin by searching for solar companies in your area. Check customer reviews, ratings, and their track record. Notable companies like **Solar Philippines** and **Philippine Solar Power Alliance** have established reputations in the market.
   
2. **Check Licenses and Certifications**: Ensure that the companies you consider are licensed and certified by the Department of Energy (DOE) and the National Renewable Energy Board (NREB). This ensures adherence to industry standards.
   
3. **Request Multiple Quotes**: Don't settle for the first quote you receive. Obtain at least three estimates to compare pricing, services offered, and warranties. A transparent company will provide detailed information about equipment and installation.
   
4. **Evaluate Experience and Expertise**: Look for companies with extensive experience in solar installation. Inquire about the number of installations they’ve completed, particularly in residential settings. Companies like **Envirosolar** demonstrate their expertise through their portfolio.
   
5. **Assess Customer Support**: Choose a company that offers excellent customer support. A reliable provider should be available to answer questions and provide assistance even after installation is complete.

## 3. Step-by-Step Installation Process: What to Expect
After selecting your solar company, understanding the installation process can help set your expectations:
- **Site Assessment**: A technician will evaluate your roof to determine the best solar panel arrangement and check for shading issues.
- **Design and Permitting**: The company will create a customized solar design for your home and handle all necessary permits.
- **Installation**: This usually takes one to three days. The installation team will mount the panels, inverter, and other components on your roof.
- **Inspection and Activation**: After installation, an inspection is conducted to ensure everything meets local regulations. Once approved, your system will be activated, and you can start enjoying your solar energy.

## 4. Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them
Many homeowners fall into traps that can lead to poor solar investments. Here are common pitfalls to avoid:
- **Ignoring Hidden Costs**: Be wary of quotes that seem too good to be true. Always inquire about potential additional costs, such as maintenance fees or equipment upgrades.
- **Not Understanding Warranties**: Ensure you clearly understand the warranties offered by your chosen provider. A solid warranty can protect your investment in the long run.
- **Choosing Based Solely on Price**: While cost is important, it shouldn’t be the only factor. Quality, service, and reliability should also weigh heavily in your decision-making process.

## 5. Your First Steps Towards Solar Installation
Ready to embark on your solar journey? Start by conducting thorough research and gathering information from various solar companies in the Philippines. Create a checklist based on the five-step blueprint and reach out to potential providers for quotes. By following this structured process, you can make an informed decision that maximizes your solar investment.

Transitioning to solar power is not just an energy choice; it’s a commitment to sustainability. Take the first step today towards a greener future by exploring your solar options.
